# 34.1-01 Summary

## Outcome

Removed umbrella remapping from the pipeline category identity layer so `TradeContext` now preserves exact parsed labels from `recap_by_category` and `trade_summary`.

## What Changed

- `trade_context.py`
  - preserves exact parsed category titles instead of remapping them into synthetic umbrella buckets
  - skips recap rollup subtotal labels such as `O&P Items`, `General Contractor O&P Items`, and `Total`
  - keeps recap subtotal categories only when they are real standalone rows such as `Overhead`, `Profit`, `Material Sales Tax`, and `Permits and Fees`
- `models.py`
  - updated category field descriptions to reflect exact parsed category identity
- `test_trade_context.py`
  - added regression coverage proving umbrella labels do not appear
  - updated existing tests to the exact-label contract

## Verification

- `PYTHONPATH=. pytest tests/test_trade_context.py -q`
- Result: `28 passed`

## Notes

The overcount regression during implementation came from treating recap rollup subtotals as real categories. That was corrected before closing this plan.
